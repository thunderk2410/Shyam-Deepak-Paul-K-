About

I'm a data scientist and maker based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. My work lives at the boundary between software and physical systems — predictive ML models, IoT sensor pipelines, edge AI deployments, and 3D-printed hardware.

I spent a year as Student Faculty in the IoT & Robotics Lab at Loyola College, mentoring students through Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and robotics. I believe learning only sticks when curiosity is intrinsic — and I design experiences around that.

Outside academia, I founded 3DPrintz, running end-to-end operations from Fusion 360 design through production and delivery. Currently interning at Aeronero Solutions, building data pipelines and dashboards for climate and AWG machinery.
